## 2. Customer-Facing Interfaces

### 2.1 QR Landing & Session (`session.html`)
- **Route**: `GET /qr/{qr_token}` -> `session.html` / `303 Redirect`
- **Purpose**: Welcomes the customer upon physical QR scan, verifies shop active status, initializes a unique `job_id`, and transitions directly into the upload stage.

### 2.2 Drag-and-Drop Document Upload (`upload.html`)
- **Route**: `GET /upload/{job_id}`
- **Features**:
  - Drag-and-drop zone with animated upload indicators.
  - Multi-file selection support (`.pdf`, `.docx`, `.png`, `.jpg`, `.jpeg`).
  - Client-side size validation against shop policy (`max_file_size_mb`).
  - Real-time progress bar for batch uploads via `XMLHttpRequest` / `Fetch`.
  - Redirects seamlessly to `/file/settings-page/{file_id}` once files are uploaded.

### 2.3 Per-File Print Settings & AI Voice Assistant (`file_settings.html`)
- **Route**: `GET /file/settings-page/{file_id}`
- **Features**:
  - **Dynamic Thumbnail Preview**: Loads document preview thumbnail generated by `pdf2image` via `GET /preview/job/{job_id}/file/{file_id}`.
  - **Copies & Duplex Controls**: Stepper buttons for copies, toggle switch for single-sided vs double-sided.
  - **Color Mode Selector**:
    - `All Black & White`
    - `All Color`
    - `Custom Mixed`: Expands custom page range input (e.g. `Color Pages: 1, 3, 5-8`).
  - **Value-Added Finishing Services**: Dynamic checkboxes for Spiral Binding, Lamination, and Stapling with live price additions.
  - **Live Dynamic Cost Recalculation**: JavaScript triggers background price calculation on input changes, updating the cost badge instantaneously.
  - **Web Speech AI Assistant (`voice_search.js`)**: Microphone button activating the browser Web Speech API. Parses spoken phrases (e.g., *"Make 3 color copies duplex"*) and automatically populates form fields.

### 2.4 Checkout & Payment Summary (`price_summary.html`)
- **Route**: `GET /file/summary/{file_id}`
- **Features**:
  - Itemized receipt table showing page counts, copies, color rate, finishing services, and subtotal/tax.
  - **Online Razorpay Button**: Invokes `Razorpay(options).open()`. Upon successful UPI/card authorization, passes signature to `/payment/verify`.
  - **Cash Payment Button**: Triggers `POST /payment/cash/{job_id}`, marking the order for cashier confirmation.

### 2.5 Live Job Queue Tracker (`job_status.html`)
- **Route**: `GET /job/tracker/{job_id}`
- **Features**:
  - Live progress stepper: `Uploaded` -> `Payment Confirmed` -> `Queued in Spooler` -> `Printing` -> `Completed`.
  - Shows current queue position (e.g. *"Position 2 in Queue — Est. 45 seconds"*).
  - Auto-polling via `setInterval()` querying `GET /jobs/{job_id}/status`.
  - When status reaches `COMPLETED`, displays celebratory animation with direct links to *"View Thermal Receipt"*.

### 2.6 Thermal Digital Receipt (`receipt.html`)
- **Route**: `GET /receipt/job/{job_id}/view`
- **Features**:
  - Styled after 80mm thermal point-of-sale receipt slips.
  - Displays Shop Name, Address, Phone, Date, Job UUID, Itemized File Breakdown, Finishing Services, Subtotal, GST, and Total Paid.
  - Includes standard CSS `@media print` rules: hides navbar and action buttons during printing to standard thermal printers.

## 3. Shop Owner Management Portal (`dashboard.html`)

The owner dashboard is a single-page responsive command center ([`dashboard.html`](file:///c:/Users/Abhilash%20S/Desktop/AI-based-QR-printing-System/cloud_server/templates/dashboard.html)) with 6 primary modular tabs:

### 3.1 Live Queue & Dispatch Tab
- Real-time table of pending, queued, and printing jobs.
- Displays customer name, page count, payment status badge, and assigned printer.
- Action buttons: "Dispatch Next", "Cancel Job", and "Mark Finished".

### 3.2 Cashier / Cash Confirmation Modal
- Live badge indicator showing count of pending walk-in cash requests.
- Modal displaying customer name, job ID, and exact amount to collect.
- "Confirm Cash & Print" button calls `POST /payment/cash-confirm/{job_id}`, immediately releasing the job to the printer.

### 3.3 Hardware & Printer Fleet Manager
- Lists all registered local printers discovered by the Print Agent.
- Displays real-time status badges (`ONLINE`, `OFFLINE`, `PRINTING`, `PAPER_JAM`).
- Toggle switches to enable/disable color, duplex, or set a printer as default.

### 3.4 Tiered Pricing Rule Editor
- Form to configure price-per-page tiers (e.g. 1-10 pages: ₹2.00, 11-50 pages: ₹1.50, 51+ pages: ₹1.00).
- Independent configurations for Black & White vs. Color and Duplex vs. Single-sided.

### 3.5 Value-Added Finishing Services Manager
- CRUD interface to add, edit, or toggle shop finishing services (Spiral Binding, Lamination, Stapling) with unit rates.

### 3.6 Business Intelligence & ML Analytics (Chart.js)
- **Daily Revenue Line Chart**: Historical revenue trends over the past 30 days.
- **Paper Consumption Doughnut Chart**: Ratio of B&W vs Color prints.
- **7-Day ML Forecast Card**: Projected revenue and volume generated by Scikit-Learn linear regression.
- **Peak Hour Analysis**: Bar chart highlighting busiest operating hours to optimize staff allocation.
